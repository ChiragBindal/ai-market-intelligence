## When does the trade happen ? 
- When buyer and seller agreed on certain price.
- Suppose buyer tells 100 and seller tell 102 will trade happen ? No
- Therefore prices move in certain way to match the order

"Both buyers and sellers repeatedly agreed that these prices were fair enough to conduct business."

Eg : ₹100 → 50,000 shares
    ₹101 → 48,000 shares
    ₹102 → 45,000 shares
    ₹103 → 300 shares
    ₹104 → 100 shares

What happened?
1. At ₹100–102:
- Buyers kept buying.
- Sellers kept selling.
- Neither side wanted to leave immediately.
- The market spent time there.

This is acceptance.

2. At ₹103–104:
Very little business happened. Why? One possible explanation is:
- Buyers thought the price was too expensive.
- Sellers weren't willing to sell enough there.
- The market quickly moved away.
This is rejection.

## POC
- POC is the price where the market facilitated the greatest amount of business.

- Point of Control (POC) – the price with the highest traded volume.
- High Volume Nodes (HVN) – price regions where the market spent a lot of time conducting business.
- Low Volume Nodes (LVN) – price regions where the market moved quickly with little trading.
- Value Area (VA) – the range containing most of the session's traded volume.
