## Initial Balance 
- Core Ideas in Auction Market Theory

Let's Start with a question :
- Imagine the NSE opens at 9:15 AM. Thousand of participants enter the market : 
Retail traders, Mutual funds, Hedge funds, HFT firms, Institutions
Q. Do you think everyone immediately agrees on "correct" price ? 
Offcourse not.

## First Hour
Market is discovering where buyers and sellers are willing to trade.
- Eg of Price Action :
9:15, 100 -> 103 -> 99 -> 104 -> 101 -> 102
During this period :
Highest Price - 104 , Lowest Price - 99
- Intial Balance High = 104 , Initial Balance Low = 99 , Everything between them is Initial Balance

## Why does it matter
- Price stays inside the Initial Balance -> balanced auction
- Price breaks above and continues -> Initiative buying.
- Price breaks above but quickly rejects -> Rejection

## Lets visualize it
Initial Balance , 104 -- IB High : 99 -- IB Low
For the first hr , market considered 99-104 to be fair auction range.
- 106 -> Lots of trading
  105 -> ........
  104 ----------- Ib High
- The market is no longer comfortable auctioning between 99 - 104
- Buyers are willing to pay above the faur range
- Sellers were also willing to transact 
- A new auction is beginning above the Initial Balance.
