## Initiative vs Responsive Activity
Goal : why trend start , why reversals happen , and why absorption occurs

First Principle - 
Every trade has : 
1. Aggressive Order (Liquidity consumer)
2. Passive Order (Liquidity Provider)

Intiatiate : One who initiate order. (Market move because of them)
Responsive : who wait for orders

Scenario 1 : Strong Uptrend : There are aggressive buyer who are willing to pay higer prices , strong uptrend
Scenario 2 : Price Remain Same : This mean sell side absorption
Scenario 3 : BreakOut : seller ran out of liquidity , there price rises

Delta : who is aggressive ? 
FootPrint : where were they aggressive ?
Imabalance : how dominant were they ?
Absorption : Did the passive side stop them ?
Breakout : Did the aggressive side finally win ?
