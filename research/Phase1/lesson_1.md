## What is stock market ?


## IPO(Initial Public Opening)
- Raises capital by selling ownership shares to investors.
- Suppose i own a company and want to raise fund/capital from investor/public , so i can list my company on stock market
- Suppose my company worth : 2cr , #no of shares : 10,000 , cost : 2000/share
- Now Suppose Investor A : 500 share , Investor B : 200 , ....... , so on
- This 2cr collected at the time of IPO goes to company account(create new 10000 share) / founder share (if founder sell its own share)

    ## Does after stock get listed on stock market , price up/ down affect it ?
    - No , it will not affect (directly)
    - Eg , suppose 1 year later price increases from 2000/share - 5000/share , Therefore valuation become 5cr , but owner will not get 3cr. Instead 3cr changes hand b/w investors
