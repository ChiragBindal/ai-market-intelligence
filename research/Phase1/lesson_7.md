## Absorption
Suppose a large buying order came but the price does not move , there may be possibility of sell side absorption

# Evidence 1 
- Aggressive buying , How we know ?
1. Positive Delta (aggressive buying volume - aggressive sell volume)
2. Buying Imbalance (buy volume(curr)/sell volume(curr+) > 3)

# Evidence 2
- Price doesn't rise even of aggressive buying.

# Evidence 3
- High volume traded

## Mathematically :
Absorption = Aggressive buying + Large volume + Price doesn't move

Q. How we decide whether the volume is high ?
We will define volumeTreshold , it is very relative depending the stock volume traded
Suppose : Stock A -> Avg daily volume : 5000 share , threshold : 1000 , thats make 20%
Stock B -> Avg daily volume : 100000 share , threshold : 1000 , this would be meaningless
Therefore , we define something relative : Observed buying volume / Expected buying volume
Is buying at this price usually high ?
